Progressive cameras show a cached preview immediately, then switch to WebRTC when the source supports it, including two-way audio where available. Other sources continue to use JPEG updates, and existing cameras keep their configuration. Each camera can use an optional preview source and has separate settings for H.264 conversion, video pacing, and PCMU audio compatibility on supported RTSP streams.

The talk button stays in the bottom right corner of the camera dialog while zooming or panning. Camera streams on iPhone and iPad start without the native playback overlay, and camera zoom no longer interferes with loading dashboard video.
